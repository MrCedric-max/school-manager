INTEGRATED SCHEME OF WORK FOR ENGLISH LANGUAGE CLASS 1 
Theme 1: The Home 
Level 1 (Primary 1) Duration: Weeks 1 to 4  
Focus Domains:  
Listening and Speaking ; Reading ; Writing ; Grammar, Vocabulary, and Pronunciation ; Phonics ; Literature and Drama ; Culminating 
Project 
General Objectives 
By the end of the theme, learners should be able to: 
• Recognize, pronounce, and trace basic letter sounds and common home-related vocabulary 
• Demonstrate polite expressions and greetings in home settings 
• Identify tools and objects in the home through words, images, and actions 
• Decode and spell monosyllabic words connected to routines and spaces 
• Compose short oral and written phrases introducing people and places at home 
• Participate in storytelling, singing, and simple role-play about life at home 
Culminating Project: My Home Snapshot Strip 
Learners will: 
• Create a strip with a drawing of their home and three labeled items 
• Add a greeting phrase and polite expression 
• Include a short sentence introducing one family member 
• Perform a rhyme or song related to life at home

Listening and Speaking; Reading; Writing; Grammar, Vocabulary and Pronunciation; Phonics; Literature and Drama; Culminating 
Project 
General Objectives 
By the end of the theme, learners should be able to: 
• Recognize common places, people, and routines in the village or town 
• Use polite expressions to greet and describe people and places in the community 
• Read and pronounce simple words related to village and town life 
• Identify and write names and locations using basic sentence structure 
• Participate in songs, stories, rhymes, and dialogue connected to daily community life 
Culminating Project: My Village/Town Strip 
Learners will: 
• Create a strip showing 3 places in their village/town with drawings and labels 
• Add short phrases describing one person and place 
• Include a polite greeting or request related to the community 
• Recite a rhyme or short chant about daily life in the village or town

Theme 8: Communication 
Level 1 (Primary 1) Duration: Weeks 29 to 32  
Focus Domains:  
Listening and Speaking ; Reading ; Writing ; Grammar, Vocabulary, and Pronunciation ; Phonics ; Literature and Drama ; Culminating 
Project 
General Objectives 
By the end of the theme, learners should be able to: 
• Identify common communication methods and tools (verbal, non-verbal, digital, and traditional) 
• Use appropriate greetings, expressions, and descriptive vocabulary during communication 
• Read, write, and decode basic words related to expressing ideas, messages, and feelings 
• Apply grammar structures including sentence types, pronouns, and punctuation for clarity 
• Build and spell words related to talking, asking, and sharing information 
• Engage in dialogue, chant, storytelling, and role-play about exchanging messages and feelings 
Culminating Project: How We Communicate Strip 
Learners will: 
• Create a strip showing two forms of communication (e.g., talking and messaging) with drawings and labels 
• Add greeting and expression phrases for sharing ideas politely 
• Include one chant or short role-play about giving and receiving a message 
• Present the strip with clear explanation and oral performance
